# Adaptive Areas for Home Assistant

[![Build Status](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/actions/workflows/validation.yaml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/actions)
[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/frandle82/adaptive-areas.svg)](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/releases)

Tired of writing the same automations, over and over, for each of your rooms? You wish Home Assistant just figured out all entities you have in an area and **magically** started being smarter about them?

Adaptive Areas is the batteries that were missing! Would like to have a single `motion` sensor that is grouped to all your other motion sensors in that area? What if all (most) of your `sensor` and `binary_sensor` entities had aggregates (grouping/average), PER AREA? Would you like for lights and climate devices to turn on and off **MAGICALLY** whenever an area is occupied/clear?

If you think all of the above features are useful, **Adaptive Areas** is here for you!

Adaptive Areas is based on [Magic Areas](https://github.com/jseidl/magic-areas).
