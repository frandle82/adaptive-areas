# Adaptive Areas for Home Assistant

[![Build Status](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/actions/workflows/validation.yaml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/actions)
[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/frandle82/adaptive-areas.svg)](https://github.com/frandle82/adaptive-areas/releases)

Tired of writing the same automations, over and over, for each room? Wish Home Assistant could understand the entities in an area and adapt to them?

Adaptive Areas provides the missing area-level building blocks: one presence result from multiple motion sensors, per-area sensor and binary-sensor aggregates, and optional light or climate control based on occupied/clear state.

If you think all of the above features are useful, **Adaptive Areas** is here for you!

Adaptive Areas is based on [Magic Areas](https://github.com/jseidl/magic-areas).
